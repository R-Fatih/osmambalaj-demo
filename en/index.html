What products does OSM Ambalaj manufacture?

add

We manufacture A-Box (regular slotted), crash-lock bottom, die-cut, tray, partitioned and export boxes, made from E, B, C and double-wall (E+B, B+C) corrugated board in the flute type that suits your product.

Do you make custom-size and printed boxes?

add

Yes. We produce our boxes to order in custom sizes with a design suited to your product and needs, and printing is available.

Which flute type should I choose?

add

E flute suits consumer and gift packaging that needs print quality, B flute suits die-cut boxes and C flute suits shipping boxes. For heavy loads and export packaging, E+B or B+C double wall is preferred. Our team will recommend the right flute type for your product.

Are your products recyclable?

add

Yes, our corrugated board products are 100% recyclable. We source raw materials locally and carry out all production in our own facility.

Where is your factory?

add

Our factory is located at Kurşunlu Mah. Yeni İstanbul Cad. No:626, Sarayönü / Konya, Türkiye, with a 5,500 m² covered production area. We have been manufacturing since 2002.
